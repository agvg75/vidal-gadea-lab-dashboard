Every figure below is computed from the roster each time this page loads. Comparisons against national averages were removed on 23 October 2026, because the benchmark ranges previously shown here could not be traced to a specific published table.

How these numbers are produced. They are computed in the browser from the lab roster file, so they change when the roster changes. Nothing on this page is typed in by hand.

Who is counted. Alumni are people who have left the lab. Current and inactive members appear in the roster and in the totals, and are excluded from alumni figures. Career continuation counts only alumni recorded as continuing in STEM or not, leaving out those still studying and those we have lost touch with.

Destinations. Many older records hold a placeholder such as "Undergraduate Researcher" rather than a destination. Those count as not recorded rather than as a known outcome.
